## Proposed isolated-engineering applicability

For F012 alone, non-clinical, policy-shaped software mechanisms may proceed through separately authorized engineering lifecycle stages without falsely recording absent professional signatures, only when:

1. Patient, product/ingredient codes, rule scenarios and reviewer designations are visibly synthetic and reviewed for the engineering purpose.
2. Deployment configuration isolates the demonstration from real-patient inputs, production credentials, professional authority stores and clinical-content publication.
3. Simulated reviewer designations cannot populate or satisfy genuine credential, clinical-owner designation, signing-grant or source-admission checks.
4. Synthetic rule results demonstrate mechanics only. They establish no medicine classification, treatment recommendation, source admissibility or clinical validation.
5. Contextual current authorization, independence, AAL/purpose where contracted, forced RLS/non-owner execution, immutable history, version binding, atomic audit/outbox/idempotency and recovery remain required.
6. Unknown or unverifiable inputs produce not_fully_checked, never an unsupported safe result.
7. Engineering verification, professional approval and production activation evidence remain separately classified.
8. Applicable OPEN gates retain their IDs, owners, closure evidence, capability restrictions and earliest blocked lifecycle stages.

This is a proposed applicability amendment for engineering-mechanism code and test vectors. It does not waive Article VI for actual clinical logic, real classifications, source approval, professional reviewer eligibility, emergency policy or clinically operational use. Product Owner approval cannot supply professional clinical/legal/facility authority.
