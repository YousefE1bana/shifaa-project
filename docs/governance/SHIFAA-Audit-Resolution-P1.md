## Additional externally verified corrections applied

1. **PDPC status, law, and regulations:** active PDPC guidelines replace the older assumption that the Center was not operational and expressly reference Executive Regulations Decision 816/2025. Law 151/2020 research copies support sensitive-data/licensing/transfer controls, but the [archived Arabic Gazette scan](https://manshurat.org/node/66932) is not on an official government host and the [English translation](https://eg.andersen.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Law-No.-151-OF-2020.pdf) is secondary. `OPEN-LEGAL-007` therefore blocks treating article mappings as production legal evidence until controlling Arabic texts and counsel validation are archived. Official operational guidance includes the PDPC [consent guideline](https://pdpc.gov.eg/assets/pdf-data/Guidelines/DSConsent.pdf) and [privacy-notice guideline](https://pdpc.gov.eg/assets/pdf-data/Guidelines/Privacy%20Notice.pdf).
2. **Production hosting:** current [Supabase regions](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/platform/regions) do not include Egypt. Production PHI therefore defaults to Egypt-resident/self-hosted infrastructure or a specifically authorized alternative, not “Supabase Cloud is acceptable” by assumption.
3. **Deprecated field-encryption dependency:** Supabase states [`pgsodium` is pending deprecation](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/database/extensions/pgsodium); the architecture now uses application envelope encryption and a separate HMAC blind index.
4. **Pharmacy-director rule:** the EDA-hosted [Law 127/1955 translation](https://www.edaegypt.gov.eg/media/4d5hhv1f/pharmacy-law-127-1955-translated.pdf) distinguishes one-institution directorship (Article 19) from pharmacy ownership limits (Article 30). The schema now models the actual distinction.
5. **Digital prescription/UHI claims:** EDA describes a digital-prescription project under coordination, and EHA reports operational e-prescriptions within UHI facilities, but no reviewed source publishes a SHIFAA-accessible national API. See [EDA project update](https://edaegypt.gov.eg/en/media-center/news/eda-explores-mechanisms-for-implementing-the-digital-prescription-system/) and [EHA digital transformation](https://eha.gov.eg/en/news/highlights-transformation/). Integration remains an adapter/open authority gate.
6. **Payments:** digital care payment is limited to a verified CBE-licensed provider and hosted/tokenized flow, following the [CBE oversight framework](https://www.cbe.org.eg/en/payment-systems-and-services/payment-systems-and-services-oversight). Donations are excluded from graduation scope and may re-enter only through the licensed-partner model in ADR-016.
7. **Accessibility:** the MVP baseline is WCAG 2.2 AA, not a deferred screen-reader/keyboard tier. The earlier “icon/color alone” rule was reversed because accessible meaning cannot depend on color/icon alone; the [W3C standard](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) is now the reference.
8. **Ambulance instruction:** the fallback now names `123`, supported by the Egyptian Ambulance Organization’s [official FAQ](https://eao.gov.eg/faq), the Egyptian Health Council’s [first-aid guidance](https://lms.ehc.gov.eg/lms/mod/book/view.php?chapterid=2482&id=454), and the NTRA [emergency-number list](https://www.tra.gov.eg/ar/atrc-faq/important-telephone-numbers/). The product still does not claim dispatch or reservation.

## Remaining blockers are not audit defects hidden as decisions

The synchronized decision/open register in PRD Section 10/Master Section 14 remains part of this resolution. The documentation does not claim a medical-retention duration, controlled-drug electronic substitution, public EDA API, UHI integration API, disability-card API/private-facility benefit, vendor DPA/SLA, signed clinical content, approved visual baseline, pinned reproducible toolchain, or generated full OpenAPI/DDL/client set that was not obtained during this review. It also does not seek donation authority for graduation because the entire donation capability is explicitly out of scope.
