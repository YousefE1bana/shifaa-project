## Boundary directions approved in principle

Yousef approved in principle the direction of a separately authored,
versioned, engineering-only synthetic prescription fixture set. Version
1.0.0 remains proposed pending review of the schema, manifest, canonical
field mapping and admissible test values.

The set must remain isolated from F011 fixtures, F013 product-catalog
ownership and production inputs. Synthetic controlled/NTI values test
mechanisms only and establish no real clinical classification or validity.
No medicine codes, clinical rules or clinical-source admissibility are
approved here.

Yousef also approved in principle the product-boundary direction that F012
owns reviewer discovery while F014 retains fulfilment-worklist ownership.

Reviewer eligibility, assignment, request-specific disclosure, emergency
queuing, endpoint design and operation count remain OPEN pending
authoritative sources and complete contract analysis. No runtime permission,
clinical qualification, assignment policy or clinical/legal approval is
created by these directions.
