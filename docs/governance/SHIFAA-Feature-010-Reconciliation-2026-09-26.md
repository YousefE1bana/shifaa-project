## 2026-09-26 specification-gate addendum — Product Owner and Architecture

This addendum resolves the referral-authority/UI, chat-membership, completion, booking, and specification-detail findings identified in the read-only Feature 010 gate review. It amends the Feature 010 specification semantics below without adding an operation, permission code, route, clinical state, or Feature 011 behavior. The original four decisions remain in force.

- **Referral authority and placement:** PAT may review and accept an own referral. GUA requires current active approved guardianship for that subject. DEL requires both current `record.view` and `appointment.manage` on that subject. `listReferrals` is role-projected for PAT/GUA/DEL/CLN, with current authority rechecked on every read and acceptance; failure denies without disclosure or booking. Source CLN requires current treating/facility/action authority. Target CLN receives only an `accepted` referral with a linked appointment at its facility under current target membership/action/purpose and only the accepted field selection. Patient `/records` owns the pending list, preview of the exact proposed disclosure, and acceptance. Clinic `/referrals` supports clinician creation and tracking only. The accepted field set remains `reason_summary` plus optional source `encounter_type`; no new permission or route is introduced.
- **Chat membership:** The subject patient participates while the linked encounter is `open` and its appointment is `in_consultation`. Workforce chat participation derives only from current active `clinical.encounter_participants` intervals. `createEncounter` establishes the initial workforce interval, and `updateEncounter` is the Feature 010 operation that ends an eligible non-responsible interval. Ending an interval immediately denies that workforce member message history and sends. GUA/DEL do not participate in Feature 010 chat. `completeEncounter` ends all context access. The later participant disposition below governs the exact initial set and defers addition.
- **Completion and booking:** Conditions, observation references, and order references each have cardinality `0..n`; their absence cannot block completion. The responsible clinician must author a nonblank summary and explicitly confirm structural completion. This confirmation does not assert medical-content sufficiency. `acceptReferral` inherits Feature 009 authoritative availability/version, slot-race, idempotency, server-priced fee, fixed EGP, and `cash_on_arrival` safeguards when it creates the linked appointment.
- **Specification completeness:** Master §11.2's normative logical fields/nullability, transitions, per-action authorization/RLS, API request/result/problem semantics, concurrency, audit/events, UI states, threats, and acceptance vectors belong in Feature 010 `spec.md` before its specification gate. `OPEN-TECH-002` defers generated full OpenAPI, physical DDL/RLS migrations, and generated clients; it does not waive those specification semantics. ## OPEN-F010-001 — linked encounter handoff

`createEncounter` requires a linked Feature 009 appointment in `checked_in` and its matching queue entry in `called`. A missing queue entry is a fail-closed invariant violation. In one transaction the operation creates exactly one `open` encounter for the appointment, advances the appointment to `in_consultation`, and advances the queue entry to `in_service`. `completeEncounter` requires that linked `open` encounter, appointment `in_consultation`, and queue `in_service`; it atomically makes all three `completed`. Encounter statuses are exactly `open` and `completed`; referral statuses are exactly `pending` and `accepted`. No Feature 010 operation creates an unlinked encounter, adds an operation ID, or changes a Feature 009 producer. State, authorization, replay, and version conflicts leave all three records unchanged.

## OPEN-F010-002 — note visibility and referral authorization

`clinical_notes.visibility_code` is exactly `private` or `patient_visible`. `private` is readable only by the currently authorized treating care team. `patient_visible` additionally permits the authorized patient, guardian, or delegate projection under current record authority. Every signed note is immutable; correction appends a signed superseding version whose signer is either the original author or the current responsible clinician, with current authorization required in both cases. No signed-note body enters a referral payload.

`createReferral` creates a `pending` internal referral, with the canonical `reason_summary` and optional source `encounter_type` as the only proposed disclosure content. It does not transmit that content to the target or imply patient authorization. `acceptReferral` is the explicit patient/guardian/delegate authorization and booking point: the actor reviews and authorizes exactly `reason_summary` plus the selected optional `encounter_type`, and the operation atomically changes the referral to `accepted`, creates the resulting appointment, and links it to the source encounter. The accepted field selection and attributable acceptance are recorded with the referral and minimum audit evidence; physical payload and persistence mapping remain `OPEN-TECH-002`. Guardian/delegate authority is checked live at acceptance; no relationship or consent is inferred. Loss of authority later blocks future actions but does not retroactively mutate the accepted referral. A failed or stale acceptance causes neither booking nor disclosure. Production lawful-basis, sensitive-data, and retention evidence remains under the existing Legal/DPO gates.

## OPEN-F010-003 — contextual chat

Feature 010 realizes appointment-context chat only while the linked encounter is `open` and its appointment is `in_consultation`. The patient composition is inside the existing `/encounters/:id` route; there is no pre-encounter chat, patient inbox, new route, or general consultation channel. Every history read and send requires current participation in that context. Participant removal or successful `completeEncounter` immediately ends both read and send access. After reconnect, the client reauthorizes and reconciles through authoritative REST and displays stale/last-updated state. This is an access lifecycle rule, not a statutory message-retention period. Order/prescription chat contexts are not realized by Feature 010.

## OPEN-F010-004 — body-only messages

`sendContextMessage` accepts a body only. Any `attachment` property, including explicit null, is rejected. `trust.messages.attachment` remains null. Feature 010 has no attachment UI or chat upload-intent operation.
