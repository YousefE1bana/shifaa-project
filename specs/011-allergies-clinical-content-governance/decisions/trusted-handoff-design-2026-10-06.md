## Normative decisions

The real Evidence Port resolves exact immutable bytes and computes/verifies SHA-256 in the application/service boundary. No JSON/escaping/locale/Unicode/whitespace normalization. workflowVersion is excluded. AAD is reconstructible from authoritative columns/constants, not a stored free-form payload.
- Trusted service allocates releaseId before protection; version candidate is 1 for initial or authoritative predecessor.artifactVersion + 1 for successor. Preflight, evidence verification and protection finish before write locks. SQL locks/revalidates lineage/context and expected version, requiring candidate equality or atomic rejection. Every rejected pre-commit version/race attempt retries outside locks with a fresh releaseId/random nonce/envelope. Committed replay uses existing authorized stored-result semantics and does not re-encrypt or create a second release.
- C03 may prove only synthetic physical storage/binding/RLS/grants/functions, artifact bytes/hash and allocation/race rejection in both runtimes. ## Necessary allocation-constraint reconciliation

The newly specified candidate version is lineage-relative, not an ordinal allocated per draft. Distinct initial drafts and fresh replacements after a rejected initial draft necessarily use version 1; competing same-predecessor drafts use the same predecessor.version + 1 under distinct release IDs. Keeping the old global draft allocator or unique(series_id, artifact_version) would make the approved competing-draft/rejected-draft replacement behavior impossible.

The executable model therefore removes the obsolete next_artifact_version draft allocator and replaces that per-series draft-ordinal uniqueness with release-scoped unique(id, series_id, artifact_version). It preserves id primary identity, immutable histories, signature resource_id/resource_version binding, unique published root per series and unique published successor per predecessor. Stale lineage/version allocation still rejects atomically under the existing lock order. This is an engineering consequence of the supplied allocation decision that preserves approved product semantics; it adds no operation, workflow state or cancellation/bypass policy.
