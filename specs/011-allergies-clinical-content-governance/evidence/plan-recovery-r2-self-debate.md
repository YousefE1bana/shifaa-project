## Pass D — Judge

The three authorized recovery corrections materially improve the architecture and resolve their specific contradictions. Static schema/vector/hash consistency is not enough to dismiss the remaining signing handoff/storage gap. R2-SD-04 affects independent exact-artifact approvals and the evidence later consumed by publication; P4 would still need to invent architecture. **Engineering Plan readiness: NOT READY. Formal lifecycle readiness: BLOCKED.**

Scope/baseline conflict: none introduced by R2. The missing signing boundary is incomplete baseline enforcement, not a new product semantic or a request to reopen the approved Spec. OPEN gates themselves are not engineering defects. Prior Plan approval remains suspended; OPEN-UX-001 separately awaits required Feature-011 Design Lead approval/designation, OPEN-TECH-002 remains open. OPEN-UX-002, OPEN-TECH-003, OPEN-CLIN-001 and OPEN-LEGAL-001/002/007 retain their stage effects and owners.

Non-blocking improvements: future implementation evidence should report vector IDs separately from planned negative obligations, and measure bounded local receipt encryption/serialization while locks are held against existing latency budgets. These are evidence/operational details, not permission to change protocols or choose hidden architecture.

Overengineering challenge: separate ARTIFACT/BODY/AAD are required for independently reproducible signing versus immutable encrypted resource identity. Allergy is a different revision resource; one complete facts payload plus optional reason avoids per-scalar protocols. Receipt is a different atomic original-response snapshot and needs separate AAD/COMMIT enforcement. Length-framing and SQL/core equivalence preserve approved deterministic bytes. Duplicate evidence digests in artifactFacts/trustedEvidence are admitted only after exact equality, not independent authorities. No simplification found that removes these boundaries while preserving all required invariants. Signing correction should be a small provider-independent handoff/sidecar definition, not a new signing service/vendor.

Files requiring further architectural correction for R2-SD-04: plan.md, research.md, data-model.md, contracts/governance-boundaries.md and a bounded internal signing handoff/profile/vector/parity definition if needed; then quickstart and current recovery/matrix/hash review records. Do not alter frozen ARTIFACT/1, public OpenAPI, approved Spec, historical Tasks or accepted historical evidence. No signing contract is invented in this round.

Fresh Tasks: inappropriate while this blocker remains; still prohibited even after technical correction until formal Plan/gate review and explicit Tasks authorization. No execution or approval occurred in this review.
