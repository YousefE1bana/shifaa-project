## 1. Problem and scope

### Problem statement

An authorized clinic team needs to document an encounter and issue a referral that discloses only the patient's authorized summary. The patient needs to see released encounter records and act on a referral. Participants also need messages tied to a valid care context, without creating an unrestricted consultation channel. This feature builds on the merged Feature 009 appointment and queue foundation, which deliberately created no encounter and no consultation-state producer.

### Actors and authorization context

| Actor | Facility/patient relationship | Permitted outcome | Explicitly prohibited |
|---|---|---|---|
| Treating clinic workforce (`CLN`) | Current named membership, treating relationship, facility, patient, purpose, and required AAL | Create/view/update authorized encounters; sign own notes or an authorized supersession; create pending internal referrals; use appointment-context chat if a participant | Arbitrary cross-facility access, viewing private notes through a safety-data share, signing outside the approved correction rule |
| Responsible clinician | Authorized encounter owner within treating team | Complete encounter with a nonblank authored summary, explicit structural confirmation and linked state guards; sign an authorized superseding note | Completing an unrelated encounter, claiming medical-content sufficiency from structural confirmation, or bypassing the linked appointment/queue handoff |
| Patient (`PAT`) | Own record and active context | View patient-visible released record, list/accept own referrals, use context message API when entitled | Reading private/unreleased notes, selecting another patient's referral |
| Guardian/delegate (`GUA`/`DEL`) | GUA: current active approved guardianship for the subject; DEL: both current `record.view` and `appointment.manage` on the subject | Review/accept a subject referral under live checks; see patient-visible record fields only where separately authorized | Inheriting blanket clinical record or Feature 010 chat authority from relationship alone |
| Context participant | Subject patient while linked encounter is open and appointment in consultation; workforce only during its active encounter-participant interval | List/send body-only messages until interval ending or encounter completion | GUA/DEL, pre-encounter/order/prescription/general chat or attachments |

### In scope

- `FR-CLINIC-006`: encounter identity, facility, time, optional `0..n` conditions/observation/order references, workforce participant intervals, signed versioned notes, responsible-clinician-authored nonblank completion summary and explicit structural confirmation, and patient/clinician projections. No independent order/observation lifecycle or additional table is authorized here. Existing operation IDs: `createEncounter`, `getEncounter`, `updateEncounter`, `signEncounterNote`, `completeEncounter`.
- `FR-CLINIC-007`: pending internal referral with reason summary and optional encounter type, role-projected PAT/GUA/DEL/CLN worklist, explicit patient/guardian/delegate authorization at acceptance, and resulting appointment linkage under Feature 009 booking safeguards. Patient `/records` owns pending review/acceptance; clinic `/referrals` owns clinician creation/tracking only. Existing operation IDs: `createReferral`, `listReferrals`, `acceptReferral`.
- `FR-FAC-006`: encrypted, participant- and context-bound body messages with expiry and loss-of-participation denial. Existing operation IDs: `listContextMessages`, `sendContextMessage`.
- Arabic-first and complete English route states, authorization/RLS negatives, idempotency/version conflicts, reconnect and stale-state behavior, privacy-safe telemetry, and the PATIENT plus REALTIME NFR profiles.

### Non-goals

- No Feature 011 allergy or clinical-content governance; no prescription creation, safety evaluation, override, medication logic, or clinical decision support.
- No general consultation chat, patient inbox/new route, pre-encounter or order/prescription chat realization, attachment upload or UI, upload-intent operation, or offline clinical write queue. Patient composition is confined to existing `/encounters/:id`; the Feature 010 TEST-ONLY functional baseline is approved, while `OPEN-UX-002` verification remains open.
- No automatic sharing of private notes, broad cross-facility record projection, implicit guardian/delegate record authority, or emergency-contact disclosure.
- No new operation IDs, route families, data domains, or production PHI authorization.

## 3. User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

### Journey J-01 — Document and release an encounter (P1)

1. Given a named, authorized treating clinician, a linked `checked_in` appointment, and its matching `called` queue entry, the clinician atomically opens one encounter and advances appointment/queue to `in_consultation`/`in_service`; a missing queue or unlinked request fails closed.
2. The clinician records permitted structured facts, optionally associates `0..n` conditions, observation references and order references, and signs a `private` or `patient_visible` note; its original author or current responsible clinician may correct it only with current authority and a signed superseding version.
3. The responsible clinician supplies a nonblank authored summary and explicit structural completion confirmation; the encounter, appointment, and queue complete atomically when current state rules hold. Missing optional conditions/observation/order references do not block completion, and structural confirmation does not certify clinical sufficiency.
4. The patient sees only the released projection at `/records` and `/encounters/:id`; an unauthorized person or facility receives no record.

### Journey J-02 — Refer with patient-authorized data (P2)

1. Given an authorized source encounter, the treating clinician creates a `pending` internal referral with `reason_summary` and optional source `encounter_type`; no target disclosure or patient authorization occurs at creation.
2. On patient `/records`, PAT reviews an own pending referral; GUA does so under current active approved guardianship, and DEL only with both current `record.view` and `appointment.manage`. Each read rechecks authority; the actor previews exactly the proposed content and explicitly authorizes it when accepting a valid target slot/facility. Clinic `/referrals` is clinician creation/tracking only.
3. Acceptance rechecks authority and Feature 009 availability/version, slot-race, idempotency, server-priced fee, fixed EGP and `cash_on_arrival`. The resulting `accepted` referral and appointment link to the source encounter atomically; failure or booking conflict leaves no partial appointment or target disclosure. Later authority loss blocks future actions without retroactively mutating the accepted referral.

### Journey J-03 — Exchange a context message (P3)

1. Given an appointment linked to an `open` encounter in `in_consultation`, the subject patient or a workforce member with an active encounter-participant interval lists and sends body-only messages within that appointment context; patient composition is only inside `/encounters/:id`. GUA/DEL do not participate.
2. `updateEncounter` ending a workforce interval or successful `completeEncounter` immediately denies existing-history reads and further sends. No pre-encounter, unrelated, order/prescription, inbox, or general consultation context is exposed by Feature 010.
3. Patient `/encounters/:id` and clinic `/messages` reconcile through authoritative REST reauthorization after disconnection and label stale content with the last-updated time. Feature 010 functional compositions are approved by the dated `OPEN-UX-001` decision; live behavior and `OPEN-UX-002` verification remain to be proved.

## 5. Domain model and invariants

### Entities and ownership

| Entity | Owning domain | Authoritative source | Lifecycle owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Encounter | Clinical | `clinical.encounters` | Responsible clinician and authorized treating team |
| Participant interval | Clinical | `clinical.encounter_participants` | Authorized encounter workflow |
| Signed note version | Clinical | `clinical.clinical_notes` | Signing clinician; prior version retained |
| Condition | Clinical | `clinical.conditions` | Authorized clinician; unknown standardization remains explicit |
| Referral | Clinical | `clinical.referrals` | Treating clinician creates; patient/authorized representative accepts |
| Context message | Trust | `trust.messages` | Current context participant; context controls visibility/expiry |
| Predecessor appointment/queue | Clinical, Feature 009 | Existing `clinical.appointments`/`clinical.queue_entries` | Feature 009 owns its recorded producers; Feature 010 owns the linked encounter start/completion transitions |

### State machine

Encounter status is exactly `open|completed`; referral status is exactly `pending|accepted`. `createEncounter` requires a linked Feature 009 appointment in `checked_in` with a matching queue entry in `called`. It atomically creates the `open` encounter and exactly one initial active workforce interval for the responsible clinician derived server-side from the linked appointment and current authorized treating-clinician context; the client supplies no workforce IDs. It advances appointment/queue to `in_consultation`/`in_service`. A missing queue is a fail-closed invariant violation. `updateEncounter` may end a current non-responsible workforce interval while the encounter is open; participant addition is deferred and its request is rejected. `completeEncounter` requires the linked `open`/`in_consultation`/`in_service` triple, a responsible-clinician-authored nonblank summary and explicit structural confirmation, then atomically makes all three states `completed`. Optional `0..n` conditions, observation references and order references cannot block it. No unlinked Feature 010 encounter is created. `createReferral` produces a `pending` internal referral; `acceptReferral` performs explicit PAT/GUA/DEL authorization and atomically produces its linked appointment and `accepted` status under Feature 009 booking safeguards. No target disclosure occurs before acceptance. Signed note correction appends an immutable signed supersession. Messages require the eligible subject patient or workforce with an active interval while the linked encounter is open/in consultation; interval ending or completion ends history read and send authority. GUA/DEL do not participate. All unspecified transitions deny.

Feature 009 remains the producer of only its own appointment/queue transitions. It never created an encounter or advanced consultation/service states; the two existing Feature 010 encounter operations now own the documented handoff and completion transitions. No Feature 009 operation, operation ID, or state vocabulary is changed.

### Invariants and concurrency

- The general data model permits nullable appointment association, but every Feature 010 creation requires a non-null linked appointment and matching called queue. At most one open encounter may link to that appointment, and appointment/queue/encounter patient and facility must agree. Missing queue, duplicate, stale state or mismatch denies the whole transaction.
- Each note has an attributable author, signed time, encrypted body, and `private` or `patient_visible` visibility. `private` stays with the currently authorized treating care team; `patient_visible` additionally permits a currently authorized patient/guardian/delegate record projection. The original author or current responsible clinician may append a signed superseding correction only with current authority. No signed-note body enters any referral.
- A `pending` referral is internal. Its only proposed disclosure content is `reason_summary` and optional source `encounter_type`. Every referral read and acceptance rechecks PAT self, GUA current active approved guardianship, or DEL both current `record.view` and `appointment.manage`. Source CLN read requires current treating/facility/action authority; target CLN read requires an `accepted` referral linked to that target facility's appointment, current target membership/action/purpose, and is limited to the accepted field selection. `acceptReferral` explicitly authorizes exactly those fields and the target booking; no inferred consent or pre-acceptance target disclosure is allowed. Later authority loss blocks future actions without rewriting an already accepted referral. Booking, status and source linkage are atomic and inherit Feature 009 availability/version, slot-race, idempotency, server fee, EGP and `cash_on_arrival` safeguards.
- Current context authorization is checked for both message history and sends. The linked patient participates while eligible; workforce requires an active encounter-participant interval created by `createEncounter` or changed only by `updateEncounter`. GUA/DEL have no Feature 010 chat grant. Interval ending or encounter completion revokes further access, without deleting prior communication history or inventing its statutory retention duration.
- `completeEncounter` requires the responsible clinician to supply a nonblank authored summary and explicit structural confirmation. The summary is a required completed-encounter fact, not a signed note or medical-content sufficiency assertion. Each condition, observation reference and order reference is optional `0..n`; absence cannot block completion.
- Every read and mutation re-evaluates current actor, relationship, facility, context, purpose, and necessary AAL. A stale token claim does not grant clinical scope.
- Every catalogued `I` mutation is idempotent; every catalogued `V` mutation requires the current version. Conflict/replay cannot duplicate clinical history, messages, audit, or outbox work.
- The same transaction records a successful domain mutation, its audit/outbox effects, and completed idempotency result where the baseline requires them. No clinical mutation is queued offline.

### `createEncounter` — `POST /v1/encounters`

CLN supplies a required linked appointment, patient, encounter type and an idempotency key, without a responsible-clinician or participant identity field. The server derives the sole initial workforce participant, the responsible clinician, from the linked appointment and current authorized treating-clinician context. The appointment must be `checked_in`, with a matching `called` queue entry; missing queue, mismatch, duplicate open encounter, stale state or client-supplied workforce identity fails closed. `201` returns the new encounter identity, `open` status, current version, linked appointment/queue identities and their `in_consultation`/`in_service` states. Success atomically creates the encounter and one responsible-clinician interval, advances both predecessors, and commits required audit/outbox/idempotency effects. The general nullable `appointment_id` column does not authorize unlinked Feature 010 creation.

### `getEncounter` — `GET /v1/encounters/{encounterId}`

The subject or authorized care team receives `200` with encounter identity, patient/facility context, type, status, times, permitted optional references, and note metadata/body only for currently authorized visibility. Private/unreleased notes are omitted for patient/guardian/delegate reads. A missing care purpose or current relationship denies access; there is no mutation or idempotency key.

### `updateEncounter` — `PATCH /v1/encounters/{encounterId}`

The treating CLN supplies permitted structured encounter facts and optional `0..n` existing condition/observation/order references against the current version, with idempotency and `If-Match`. This is the Feature 010 mutation for ending an active non-responsible workforce `encounter_participants` interval after explicit confirmation; it requires current treating/facility/action authority and increments the encounter version. It cannot add a participant or end the responsible clinician's interval while open. `200` returns the updated authorized encounter projection and version. A stale version, cross-patient reference, participant-add property, duplicate/invalid interval or `completed` encounter leaves all affected records unchanged; this operation creates no later-domain observation or order lifecycle.

### `signEncounterNote` — `POST /v1/encounters/{encounterId}/notes`

The treating CLN supplies type, nonblank body, explicit `private` or `patient_visible` visibility, and optional `supersedes_id` for a correction; an attributable encrypted signed note version is appended under an idempotency key. `201` returns note identity, encounter, author, signed time, visibility and supersedes link with the body only in an authorized projection. A correction is a signed superseding version of the same encounter by the original author or current responsible clinician, with current authority required for either; no prior signed version is overwritten. Private notes stay with the currently authorized treating care team; patient-visible notes additionally enter the authorized patient/guardian/delegate record projection. No signed-note body enters a referral or medication-safety share.

### `completeEncounter` — `POST /v1/encounters/{encounterId}/complete`

Only the responsible clinician supplies an authored summary that remains nonblank after whitespace trimming and an explicit true structural completion confirmation, using idempotency and `If-Match`. The linked encounter, appointment and queue must currently be `open`, `in_consultation` and `in_service`; success atomically stores the completion summary, makes all three `completed` once, and returns their identities, completed states, versions and UTC completion time with `200`. No condition, observation reference or order reference is required. Missing summary/confirmation, stale version or missing clinical authority denies without partial effects; successful completion immediately ends all chat access. Structural confirmation makes no claim of clinical sufficiency.

### `createReferral` — `POST /v1/encounters/{encounterId}/referrals`

The treating clinician supplies target specialty/facility/doctor as applicable, nonblank canonical `reason_summary` and optional source `encounter_type`. Idempotency creates only a `pending` internal referral. `201` returns referral identity, pending status/version, source encounter and proposed fields only to the authorized creating team. It does not transmit the proposed content to the target or infer patient authorization. No condition, observation, order, or signed-note body is copied into the referral payload.

### `listReferrals` — `GET /v1/referrals`

PAT may read own referrals; GUA requires current active approved guardianship for the subject; DEL requires both current `record.view` and `appointment.manage`. Source CLN requires current treating/facility/action authority; target CLN requires an `accepted` referral linked to its target-facility appointment and current target membership/action/purpose. These facts are independently rechecked on every read. `200` returns `{data, meta}` with role-projected, cursor-paginated referrals filtered only within the actor's allowed patient/facility/specialty/status/date scope; pending subject preview includes only `reason_summary` and selected optional `encounter_type`, status and permitted target/slot context. Patient `/records` owns that preview. `pending` content remains internal to source care and authorized subject review; the target sees none until acceptance and never sees more than the accepted field selection. An unrelated facility or revoked representative receives no referral content.

### `acceptReferral` — `POST /v1/referrals/{referralId}/accept`

PAT self, GUA under current active approved guardianship, or DEL with both current `record.view` and `appointment.manage` explicitly authorizes the displayed `reason_summary` and selected optional source `encounter_type` and supplies the target slot/facility under idempotency and `If-Match`. Every acceptance rechecks those facts and pending status. The target booking uses authoritative Feature 009 availability and current schedule/slot version, eligibility and exclusion checks, one-winner slot race handling, server-priced nonnegative fee in fixed EGP, and `cash_on_arrival`; the client cannot choose a fee, currency or payment method. `200` returns the `accepted` referral identity/version, exact authorized field selection and linked confirmed appointment identity/status with server-priced EGP cash terms. Success atomically records attributable acceptance, selected fields and minimum audit evidence, discloses only those fields to the target, and links the appointment to the source encounter. Physical evidence mapping remains `OPEN-TECH-002`. Slot conflict, stale version, mismatched displayed fields or missing current authority causes neither booking nor disclosure. Later authority loss blocks future actions but does not retroactively mutate the accepted referral.

### `listContextMessages` — `GET /v1/contexts/{contextType}/{contextId}/messages`

Only the subject patient or workforce member with an active encounter-participant interval for the appointment linked to an `open` encounter in `in_consultation` receives a `200` `{data, meta}` cursor-paginated authorized message projection. GUA/DEL are denied. Reauthorization occurs on every history read; interval ending or successful `completeEncounter` immediately denies further reads, including prior history. Order/prescription and pre-encounter contexts are outside Feature 010.

### `sendContextMessage` — `POST /v1/contexts/{contextType}/{contextId}/messages`

Only the subject patient or workforce member with an active encounter-participant interval for the linked appointment context while its encounter is `open` and appointment `in_consultation` sends a nonblank body with an idempotency key. GUA/DEL are denied. `201` returns the new message identity, context, sender and UTC sent time in an authorized projection. Any `attachment` property, including explicit null, is rejected; the encrypted message persists a null attachment. Interval ending or completion immediately blocks sends. Offline sends are blocked; patient composition is only within existing `/encounters/:id` and its TEST-ONLY functional composition is covered by the approved Feature 010 `OPEN-UX-001` baseline.

- **AC-02 — record projection:** Given PAT self, GUA with current active approved guardianship, DEL with current `record.view`, and an unrelated clinic actor, when each reads the same encounter, then only currently authorized actors see released fields and the unrelated actor sees no clinical content; private notes remain absent from every subject/representative projection.
- **AC-03 — signed note visibility:** Given private and patient-visible note versions, when the original author or current responsible clinician signs a correction under current authority and the patient reads, then immutable prior versions persist and private content is absent from patient and referral projections. An unauthorized signer is denied.
- **AC-04 — completion/version/offline:** Given linked `open` encounter/`in_consultation` appointment/`in_service` queue, when the responsible clinician supplies a nonblank authored summary and explicit structural confirmation, completion atomically makes all three `completed` and ends chat access even with zero condition/observation/order references. Missing/blank summary, absent confirmation, stale version, invalid source state or offline clinic client causes no partial state/audit/outbox effect; after refresh an authorized current request can proceed. No medical-content sufficiency is asserted.
- **AC-05 — referral creation:** Given a source encounter, creating a referral yields only an internal `pending` record with `reason_summary` and optional `encounter_type`; patient `/records` shows the exact pending preview only to a currently authorized subject actor, clinic `/referrals` shows clinician creation/tracking only, and no target disclosure or patient authorization occurs yet.
- **AC-06 — referral denial:** Given an attempted note/condition/observation/order field, GUA without current active approved guardianship, DEL lacking either `record.view` or `appointment.manage`, revoked authority between preview and acceptance, or an unaccepted referral, then every affected read/accept denies without pending clinical content reaching the target or an appointment being booked.
- **AC-07 — referral acceptance race:** Given explicit PAT/GUA/DEL authorization of the displayed exact reason summary and selected optional encounter type and one valid target slot, concurrent acceptance/booking produces at most one `accepted` referral and linked appointment under Feature 009 authoritative availability/version, slot exclusion, server-priced fee, fixed EGP and `cash_on_arrival`. A stale slot/version, duplicate request with changed body, client-supplied fee/currency/payment mode or lost authority causes no booking/disclosure; same-key identical replay returns its stored result. Later authority loss blocks future actions but does not rewrite the accepted result.
- **AC-08 — context expiry:** Given a subject patient and current workforce interval, when `updateEncounter` ends that interval after a message read, the workforce member's next history read/send denies immediately while the eligible patient remains allowed. GUA/DEL always deny. Successful `completeEncounter` then denies every actor's history read/send, with no new message or retrospective deletion.
- **AC-09 — replay/cross-scope:** Given same-key different-body and cross-facility requests, when each mutation is attempted, then a stable conflict/denial occurs with no duplicate clinical effects or disclosure.
- **AC-10 — body-only and recovery:** Given any `attachment` property (including null) or a disconnected client, sending is rejected or blocked with no attachment persisted. After reconnect, patient `/encounters/:id` and clinic `/messages` reauthorize/reconcile through REST and show the last-update/stale label.
- **AC-11 — bilingual accessibility and redaction:** Given every approved Feature 010 route/state in Arabic and English, when used by keyboard and screen reader at required sizes, then controls/states are intelligible and prohibited clinical text is absent from telemetry.
- **AC-12 — optional clinical references and signed correction:** Given an encounter with zero condition/observation/order references, an authorized update may associate `0..n` existing same-subject references without creating those resources; absent references do not block completion. Original author or current responsible clinician under current authority appends a same-encounter signed supersession, preserving the prior encrypted version and its visibility history; an unrelated signer or cross-patient reference denies.
- **AC-13 — referral read/route scope:** Given PAT, active approved GUA, DEL with both required current grants, DEL with only one grant, and unrelated CLN, each `/records` referral list/preview read is independently authorized and role-projected; the one-grant DEL and unrelated actor receive no referral content. Clinic `/referrals` provides creation/tracking but no acceptance control. A revoked grant between pages denies the next page.
- **AC-14 — chat membership and reconnect:** Given an open linked encounter, `createEncounter` establishes only the server-derived responsible-clinician interval. The clinic encounter UI reviews active/historical intervals and confirms ending an active non-responsible interval through `updateEncounter`; it cannot add a participant or end the responsible clinician's open-encounter interval. Subject PAT and active workforce can list/send in appointment context, while GUA/DEL and ended workforce deny immediately after interval end. Following disconnect, both patient `/encounters/:id` and clinic `/messages` reconcile through REST and display stale/last-updated state; a pushed event alone grants no access.
