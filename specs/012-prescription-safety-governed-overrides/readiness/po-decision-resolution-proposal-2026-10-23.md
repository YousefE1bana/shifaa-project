## A. Q1-U1 — evidence envelope

**SOURCE_DERIVED:** a successful API response, complete traversal of a declared source scope, coherent evidence usable by the selected mechanism, and clinically sufficient evidence are four distinct claims.

The logical envelope records: subject and authorized reader/action/facility/purpose; synthetic environment; producer identity and permitted projection; declared source/filter/status/history scope; current/history view where supported; page completion; returned assertion/item identities and revisions; source version/digest and actual timestamps/stale signals if supplied; known coverage and explicit gaps; prescription/item/check binding; and separate mechanism outcome versus overall clinical `not_fully_checked`. An absent attribute remains unknown, not invented. F011 status metadata never supplies a rule body or a new safety-consumer grant. S2–S6 require these distinctions; this is not a new DTO.

Technical completeness levels, used in the matrix:

- **T0:** required response/evidence absent, failed, stale or incomplete; no usable current complete mechanism proof.
- **T1:** all pages for the authorized declared read were obtained, final cursor is null where supplied, required response fields exist, each page reauthorizes and source metadata is attributable. This proves only that bounded read. F011 cursors promise stable order/context, not a frozen collection snapshot; `stale=false` is not an allergy-free or global freshness guarantee.
- **T2:** T1 plus the selected approved synthetic scenario's required input set, identities/revisions/digests and completeness are coherently bound and still current at action time. Revision consistency must be established, never inferred from pagination or timestamps. An unknown cross-page/current-snapshot relationship is explicitly unknown until the pre-Plan consumption design proves it. T2 does not imply clinical sufficiency.

**Draft D (SOURCE_DERIVED):** an authorized unsigned create/update may produce a truthful incomplete result; incomplete clinical assessment alone is not a universal draft prohibition. If the operation cannot produce its required safety result at all, apply §B: fail with no partial successful create/update. Already committed drafts remain available for authorized correction.

**Sign G (SOURCE_DERIVED):** separate prescriber action, current AAL2/care/context/isolation, current bound mechanism evidence and required acknowledgement/independent pathway approvals, no stale/revoked/expired/unsupported proof, and atomic required effects. T1 alone never grants signing. Actual clinical signing is outside this review regardless of technical completeness.

**Clinical U (PROFESSIONAL_INPUT_REQUIRED):** real clinical sufficiency is not established by any row below. Overall real clinical assessment remains `not_fully_checked`. There is no allergy-free attestation, medication coverage guarantee, clinical recent interval or freshness duration here.
