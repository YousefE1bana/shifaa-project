## 2. P1 — bounded assignment policy

Sources: [reviewer invariant / R2](../contract-amendment/reviewer-contract-v0.1.0.md#L66), [spec AC-08/10](../spec.md), [current draft C2](../research.md#2-architecture-completeness-decision-matrix). These require current bounded authority and valid-holder protection but do not select five/thirty minutes. The recommendation bounds stale holds in an isolated engineering exercise while allowing a deliberate review interval; no measured usability or clinical safety claim supports the numbers yet.

- Claim starts an immutable nonrenewable assignment interval. Earlier designation expiry, request expiry or current revocation terminates eligibility. Absence of a scheduled designation end does not remove the five-minute cap. Current person/account/facility/lane/action/purpose/AAL2 checks continue at each action; lease validity is necessary, never sufficient.
- Request expiry is explicit, at most thirty minutes from creation. Product must not treat this maximum as a clinical response deadline or prescription expiry. An earlier prescribed/request-bound expiry still governs.
- No renewal action, heartbeat extension, retry extension, reread extension or virtual-clock reset exists. An original-key replay reports its original outcome subject to current disclosure authorization, without claiming new authority.
- A current valid holder may release_own. For a live pending lane, a current eligible independent actor may claim after release or takeover_invalid after authoritative proof of invalidity. A held-valid assignment cannot be stolen because another actor waited or holds a stale version. Terminal requests/lanes never reopen.
- A revoked actor cannot use release as a bypass. Another eligible actor's existing recovery actions must prove current invalidity. Loss of an approved binding invalidates future reliance; it does not authorize a second decision on a terminal lane. A fresh evidence-bound linked request, where existing operations allow, supplies recovery.
- Boundary equality is expired. Claim-first versus revocation-first, release-first versus decide/sign-first, and takeover versus original holder are resolved by the accepted participating protocol and CAS; a local timestamp calculation cannot prove remote revocation ordering.

API/Data/Security must separately accept the authoritative-time and current-writer proof before this becomes accepted architecture. Any usability change to numbers must be attributable and update all affected contracts; no incidental tuning.

**Ready-to-copy P1 decision:** “Approve only the isolated engineering five-minute nonrenewable assignment cap and explicit request expiry at most thirty minutes, each bounded earlier by actual current authority/request/designation expiry as described in §2. This approves numeric Product policy, not its unproven currentness protocol, clinical timing or implementation.”

## 3. P2 — replacement contract delta and atomic outcome

Sources: [Catalog create/sign/cancel](../../../docs/architecture/SHIFAA-API-Catalog.md#L152), [Data/RLS immutable signed lineage](../../../docs/architecture/SHIFAA-Data-RLS.md#L157), [spec AC-12/16](../spec.md), [draft correction proposal](../data-model.md#4-state-and-clock-transitions).

The Catalog assigns create items/substitution/expiry and separate sign/cancel; it does not establish an approved optional predecessor wire field. The untracked OpenAPI draft is not an approved request schema. Adding predecessor intent and its concurrency precondition materially expands the proposed request/authorization contract: **formal API owner acceptance/successor is required**, whether completing an absent exact baseline or amending an existing accepted one. Product intent alone cannot approve wire compatibility.

Proposed logical clauses, subject to those owners:

1. An explicit replacement intent identifies one predecessor plus its expected current aggregate version on existing createPrescription. Both are supplied together or neither. API/Data select exact names/typing/error mapping; no new If-Match obligation on create is silently inferred from other operations. A draft with neither remains independent.
2. Only a current responsible treating prescriber with independently established correction authority for that predecessor may designate it, within the same patient and authorized originating scope. Read permission alone is insufficient; if existing action authority cannot be proven, this correction authorization remains a separate contract-owner decision. No capability is inherited from possession of a reference or another clinician's identity.
3. Draft creation does not change the predecessor. Multiple pending replacement drafts may express nonbinding intent; none reserves, cancels or supersedes a signed predecessor. The predecessor must still be the expected current signed, unexpired, uncancelled, unsuperseded resource at replacement signing. A stale binding fails, rather than silently retargeting.
4. The separate guarded replacement sign rechecks current authority/evidence/approvals, predecessor version and eligibility. Exactly one transaction commits new signed history/signature, predecessor superseded status, immutable lineage, approved P3 effects if selected, required audit/outbox/obligation/receipt. Every required failure leaves predecessor and replacement pre-sign states intact. No partially committed successful replacement.
5. Competing replacement signers cannot each supersede the same predecessor: first valid guarded commit wins; the other receives the accepted conflict outcome with no new signed replacement or source statement. Cancel-first likewise prevents replacement sign. If replacement sign committed first, later cancellation acts only on its explicitly authorized current resource and cannot overwrite lineage.
6. Existing unsigned update/check/request operations may prepare a deliberate correction after conflict with fresh evidence; they never rewrite signed content or change the predecessor reference by accident. Exact updateability of the proposed binding must be accepted by API/Data; absent such acceptance it is immutable in that draft. No hidden replacement endpoint.
7. Predecessor/successor histories and original signature bytes remain immutable. Each linked resource requires independent current read authority; a readable lineage edge is not access to its target. P/M have no new lineage disclosure under D3.

Required owner acceptance: exact correction actor/action scope; wire delta and paired precondition; whether an authorized draft correction may replace the binding; one-successor invariant, atomic status/history/event semantics and cancellation/expiry race ordering; closed error/response contracts. Actual transaction/schema choices remain owner proposals, not edits made here.

**Ready-to-copy P2 decision:** “Approve deliberate replacement intent on existing createPrescription and supersession only within successful separate replacement signing under §3, subject to separately accepted correction authority, predecessor-version wire contract, one-successor/CAS and atomic lineage. No current wire field or backend permission is approved by this Product decision.”

If carrier/authority is rejected, preserve required correction scope and record **BLOCKED_MISSING_AUTHORITY**; do not silently omit supersession or convert another field into a predecessor pointer.

## 4. P3 — prescribed source ownership and exactly-once effects

Sources: [Catalog sign and medication read](../../../docs/architecture/SHIFAA-API-Catalog.md#L158), [Data/RLS statement model](../../../docs/architecture/SHIFAA-Data-RLS.md#L66), [atomic reviewer contract](../contract-amendment/reviewer-contract-v0.1.0.md#L85), [draft proposed entities](../data-model.md#2-proposed-entities-and-constraints).

The existing medication-statement model permits prescribed as a state but does not assign this precise trigger or authorize a newly installed writer. The proposal is compatible with that logical model **conditional on explicit API/Data/Security source assignment**; it is not proof of an existing producer.

- Proposed producer: the existing Core API clinical sign operation's authoritative transaction, not a worker, new app, independent release service or thirteenth operation. Source ownership records F012 prescription/sign origin, exact immutable signed item/version and originating prescription version; user-facing provenance remains understandable.
- The uniquely committed signed item/version defines the immutable source identity. Exactly one legitimate prescribed record exists for it. Same-key replay returns the canonical authorized receipt and creates no extra statement, signature, audit/outbox or obligation effect. Different-key concurrent sign attempts still obey aggregate CAS and source uniqueness.
- Draft create/update/check/request/review alone emits no prescribed statement. Failed sign, source-currentness failure, failed predecessor supersession or failure of any required audit/outbox/receipt effect yields no new statement and no successful sign. A timeout must reconcile the original key rather than assume rollback or emit from a second channel.
- Record domain facts and provenance in the same accepted sign outcome. Outbox delivery/retry/dead-letter handling transports minimum facts and never becomes the domain producer, re-signs, emits duplicates or grants patient access. Event naming, required consumer and immutable event/receipt structure require API/Data/Platform/Security acceptance.
- If P2 is accepted, a replaced item's old prescribed statement remains its true historical source fact; no discontinued/active/completed transition is inferred. F014 alone owns actual fulfilment/dispense facts when genuinely supplied. Unknown producers for other statement statuses stay unknown.
- P3 supplies no release predicate, patient-positive journey or reviewer browsing grant. Each statement's reader/source/status/field predicate and any explicit future release require independent acceptance.

Owner acceptance must demonstrate one sign transaction boundary, uniqueness identity, authorized producer/write path, failure/replay/rollback outcomes and actual provenance/source projections. Later authorized harness verifies independent sign races, every required write failure and raw non-owner RLS; this pass executes none.

**Ready-to-copy P3 decision:** “Approve F012 sign as the proposed producer of exactly one prescribed statement for each uniquely committed signed item/version in the same atomic outcome, under §4. Require separate API/Data/Security acceptance of writer authority and contracts; infer no other medication state or patient release.”
