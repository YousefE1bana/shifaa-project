## 4. Proposed additive clauses S1–S8

If accepted through the actual governed successor process, these would supplement F011/F012 permitted-consumption contracts without changing F011 historical bytes.

1. **S1 — scope and provenance.** Every relied-on envelope identifies exact producer/contract version, patient/source scope, projection/filters, authoritative item/head/release revisions, pagination coverage, observed source time and unknown freshness/coverage separately. No clinical lookback interval or allergy-free assertion is selected. Required scope is producer/requirement-defined; arbitrary filters cannot prove it. History is not automatically the current set.
2. **S2 — completeness.** T1 means every contracted page/required source and revision condition was satisfied, including zero results within a defined scope. It never means clinically complete or no allergies/medications. T2 requires approved coherent synthetic scenario and all G guards. Complete public metadata is not an approved clinical rule.
3. **S3 — authoritative admission.** Replica/cache reads may inform an explicitly stale/partial view, but cannot authorize reliance without the authoritative producer protocol. At each evidence-reliant action, compare the captured exact source envelope under the accepted fence and retain that protection through the guarded effect commit. Recompute/refresh outside locks when required. No KMS/network work under database locks.
4. **S4 — consumer authority delta requiring approval.** The prescriber uses only independently existing F011 native read authority. For an assigned F012 reviewer lacking native F011 read authority, propose only case-bound validation of already lawfully captured permitted evidence under that reviewer's current authenticated person/case/lane/facility/purpose. This is a **new participating validation permission**, not an existing grant: F011/API/Security and Product boundary approval must explicitly authorize it before use. Its result may permit/reject current reliance, never return new assertions, rule material, unrelated source existence or a patient browsing capability. If rejected, affected reviewer actions remain blocked; borrowing the prescriber's identity is forbidden.
5. **S5 — writer participation and clocks.** Every admitted scope-changing writer must conflict/advance proof in the same authoritative effect as its source change. Include new assertions, head corrections/verification, publication/new applicable series/supersession, governed basis replacement/revocation and native/roster authority changes. Where no writer occurs at expiry/effective time, evaluate real producer-authoritative current time at guarded admission/effect; a generation cannot hide clock-driven invalidation. Owners must prove the smallest critical section and actual compatible order, not adopt the draft F012 rank table as fact.
6. **S6 — linearization.** Establish one documented guarded-effect ordering and atomic F012 commit for domain/decision/signature history, obligation, audit, minimum outbox, canonical response and completed idempotency. In a demonstrated shared database protocol, relevant producer writer first makes old reliance fail; protected action first may commit historical effect, with subsequent reliance invalid after the change. An irreversible external revocation-first observation must not be hidden by a local snapshot. Every source outside this domain needs its own accepted ordering proof; otherwise no successful dependent action.
7. **S7 — fail closed and recovery.** Missing/partial/stale/unavailable required source, cursor mismatch, changed generation/head/eligibility, absent permission or unknown current authority cannot authorize simulated signing or a current decision. Required create/update/check producer failure yields no partially successful mutation; earlier committed drafts/history remain intact. A truthful incomplete result may be stored only when the contracted producer actually produced it. Network timeout is not proof of rollback: original-key reconciliation must authorize current receipt disclosure and return original committed outcome once. New authoritative change invalidates future reliance, never rewrites history.
8. **S8 — isolation and disclosure.** No source-body fallback, online service-role/BYPASSRLS, new public operation/role or professional-signature lookup. R1 discovery remains minimal; only current own-assignment necessary detail; aggregate CAS refresh never silently rebinds evidence. No unapproved executable scenario/vector is created. F014 history remains missing where absent.
