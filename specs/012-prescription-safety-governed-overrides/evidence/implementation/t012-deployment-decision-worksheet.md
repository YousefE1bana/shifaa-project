# T012 deployment-owner decision worksheet

Use this worksheet with the [effective writer manifest](t012-effective-writer-manifest.md). It asks for intended synthetic topology and operational ownership. After a decision, engineering independently verifies effective connection roles, grants, functions, triggers, predicates, schedules and lock ordering. Provide a non-secret configuration or runbook reference and an accountable owner. The worksheet records no decisions as granted.
