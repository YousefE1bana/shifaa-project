## 3. Single source/transaction/linearization contract

**A-DB selected; no distributed transaction.** F011/F010/F012 permitted sources, current local authority and required atomic effects must participate on the same authoritative PostgreSQL connection. Existing independent adapter callbacks require future transaction composition; nested callbacks are not evidence of sharing.

Selected order: (0) durable deployment admission; (1) idempotency actor/operation/resource/key; (2) exact native user SHARE then session SHARE; (3) producer-generation rows ordered by producer/scope; (4) F011 series advisory/domain/head/revision in existing F011 order; (5) F012 prescription/predecessor UUID order → request → pharmacist then second-physician lane → assignment; (6) existing encounter/workforce/relationship/patient/person/consent/participant/governed-authority and roster ranks; (7) release/policy target-control rows ordered by target kind and UUID; (8) append domain obligation/audit/minimal outbox/canonical receipt and complete idempotency. No lower-rank discovery, upgrade, SKIP LOCKED source admission or network under locks. A late lower-rank dependency aborts/restarts.

All participating mutations take exclusive admission **before** their original locks, including source additions/head changes, content series creation/sign/publication/supersession/eligibility, F011 offline evidence/grant/designation writers, F010 encounter/participant lifecycle, facility/workforce/licence/scan changes, person/family/consent/permission append/revoke, F007 local recovery restrictions, F012 roster/release policy/withdrawal/P3 writes. They mutate source and increment the affected initialized generation in the same transaction. Missing generation/scope/epoch denies, including zero-to-first-row. The inherited writer inventory identifies exact existing SQL/adapters; admitted implementation must enumerate actual effective grants/functions/triggers/provisioners. Owner/restore/bootstrap tools either participate or run with sensitive exposure disabled/drained. No unrestricted owner work can coexist with a claimed fence.

Native GoTrue writers do not honor admission; their **real native row conflicts** are §4's separate protection. Other external sources are not silently admitted. Unknown writer/source/configuration invalidates admission and blocks affected reliance.

Reads/captures take shared admission, remain bounded, never upgrade or write an idempotency row. A mutation captures/revalidates under its exclusive admission; any earlier shared capture is immutable input only and must be revalidated. Complete scan limit is 10,000 members per producer scope, five-second total action/capture budget and two-second lock wait, at most three total confirmed-aborted attempts within that budget. Exhaustion fails the required operation, never successful truncated evidence. These are reviewable engineering limits, not measured NFR or clinical thresholds.

Producer-owned generations bind deployment/epoch/producer/scope/contract version, complete declared membership/current head/revision and allowed projection. Generation is not clinical completeness. Verified-empty means exact complete declared set contains zero records; never allergy-free/medication-free. T2+G requires separately approved coherent mechanism context, actual current authority/evidence and all signing guards. Overall assessment stays `not_fully_checked`. W-M/W-NA/N/E are logical contexts, not inferred from immutable fixture flags. Executable vectors still require their own acceptance.

READ COMMITTED fresh reads after waits; guarded conditional effect after current authority/evidence/CAS is logical action ordering. Recheck fresh clock before effect and receipt finalization, owning effective-from/expiry semantics and native JWT/TOTP boundaries; no claim physical WAL commit necessarily precedes a wall-clock expiry. Clock-driven content eligibility is evaluated without requiring a cleanup writer. Writer-first invalidates old reliance; action-first preserves only its committed historical effect. Evidence fingerprint remains distinct from aggregate CAS.

Create/update/check required-producer failure rolls back the entire required outcome; previous committed draft stays intact. Successfully produced incomplete evidence is not producer execution failure. Domain/assignment/decision/signature/P2 lineage/P3 statements/emergency obligation/audit/outbox/canonical response/completed idempotency commit or roll back together. Failure does not create successful receipts or approval. Unknown commit reconciles the original key under current authority, never assumes rollback; changed body conflicts. Same-key disclosure reauthorizes; read/replay cannot revive a request, extend a lease/window or emit effects twice.
